# SONICS

---

## 1. Summary

- SONICS is a set of **real and AI-generated songs** – music with vocals, not speech – for detecting fake songs. The paper gives *"97,164 songs (4,751 hours), including 49,074 end-to-end synthetic songs (1,971 hours) generated by Suno and Udio, alongside 48,090 real songs (2,780 hours) curated from YouTube"* (main paper, p. 2).
- **Only the fake songs are distributed as audio** (49,074 MP3 files in ten ZIP archives). The real songs come only as metadata with a YouTube ID: *"This dataset contains **only fake songs**."* (`README.md`).
- **Fake songs** come from five generators – Suno v2, v3, v3.5 and Udio 32, Udio 130 – and are of three types (Full, Mostly, Half Fake), depending on whether the lyrics and the music style are AI-generated or taken from real songs (p. 4).
- **Real songs** were found on YouTube from the metadata of the Genius Lyrics Dataset: 9,096 artists (p. 4).
- **Splits:** train 66,709, valid 4,440, test 26,015 songs. Train has only Suno v3.5 and Udio 130; the other three generators appear only in valid and test (*"unseen algorithms"*, p. 7). About half of the songs are fake (50.5 %).
- **The length of a song depends on the generator:** all Udio 32 songs last 32.9 s and all Udio 130 songs 131.2 s; Suno v2 up to 80.1 s, Suno v3 up to 120.1 s, Suno v3.5 and real songs up to 240.1 s (metadata files).
- The paper trains its detectors (among them **SpecTTTra**, the model of the paper) on SONICS with 5 s or 120 s of audio. The six public checkpoints of SpecTTTra are linked in section 3. Licence of the dataset: **CC BY-NC 4.0** (non-commercial).

## 6. Main paper describing the database

The main paper (link 3 in section 2) introduces the SONICS dataset and the model **SpecTTTra** (Spectro-Temporal Tokens Transformer) for long audio. It compares SpecTTTra with ConvNeXt, ViT and EfficientViT on 5 s and 120 s of audio. The PDF header says *"Accepted at ICLR 2025"*. The dataset is described in section 3.1 and Table 3 (pp. 3–5), the results are in Table 4 (p. 9).

Rahman, M. A., Hakim, Z. I. A., Sarker, N. H., Paul, B., & Fattah, S. A. (2025).
*SONICS: Synthetic or not – identifying counterfeit songs* (Version 4) [Preprint]. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14080
(DOI and pages: not stated in the PDF or on the arXiv page)

**How the detectors were trained:**

- Data: *"We conduct all experiments using the proposed SONICS dataset, which is divided into train, valid, and test sets."* (p. 7). Suno v2, Suno v3 and Udio 32 are *"unseen algorithms during training"* (Table 4, p. 9).
- Input: *"we resampled both real and fake songs to 16kHz"*; 5 s or 120 s. *"Any song shorter than input length is zero-padded randomly, while for longer songs, a random crop is used."* At test time the middle part is used and padding is added on the right (p. 8).
- Training from scratch, 50 epochs; main metric F1, fake songs = positive class (p. 8; Table 4).

**Results on the test split** (Table 4, p. 9; F1, and the share of detected fake songs per generator; † = unseen in training):

| Input | Model | Suno v2† | Suno v3† | Suno v3.5 | Udio 32† | Udio 130 | F1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 s | ConvNeXt | 0.62 | 0.99 | 0.99 | 0.62 | 0.99 | 0.90 |
| 5 s | SpecTTTra-α | 0.68 | 0.99 | 0.99 | 0.26 | 0.99 | 0.80 |
| 5 s | SpecTTTra-β | 0.61 | 0.98 | 0.99 | 0.18 | 0.99 | 0.78 |
| 5 s | SpecTTTra-γ | 0.51 | 0.98 | 0.99 | 0.10 | 0.99 | 0.76 |
| 120 s | ConvNeXt | 0.77 | 0.99 | 0.99 | 0.95 | 1.00 | 0.96 |
| 120 s | SpecTTTra-α | 0.78 | 0.99 | 1.00 | 0.96 | 1.00 | **0.97** |
| 120 s | SpecTTTra-β | 0.87 | 0.99 | 1.00 | 0.62 | 0.99 | 0.92 |
| 120 s | SpecTTTra-γ | 0.98 | 0.99 | 1.00 | 0.37 | 1.00 | 0.88 |

- *"all detection models achieve better performance on seen algorithms (Suno v3.5 and Udio 130) compared to unseen ones (Suno v2, Suno v3, and Udio 32)"*, and *"real songs are more easily identified than fake ones"* (p. 8).
- The paper also compares AASIST (F1 0.91) and Wav2Vec2 + AASIST (0.90) at 5 s; at 120 s they *"face out-of-memory error"* (Table 9, p. 23).
- **Public checkpoints:** the six SpecTTTra models in section 3 (α, β, γ × 5 s, 120 s). Their model cards name `awsaf49/sonics` as the training data and 16 kHz as the sample rate, but not the split. For `alpha-5s` and `gamma-5s` the GitHub table gives other values than Table 4. **The two checkpoints used in this project are not chosen yet.**

## 7. Dataset content: recordings and splits

_Based on the main paper (pp. 2–8, section 3.1, Table 3) and on the metadata files._

**Real songs:** *"This portion of the dataset was initially compiled by random sampling from the Genius Lyrics Dataset (J., 2023), which provides metadata including lyrics, titles, and artist information. Subsequently, leveraging this metadata, a search was conducted dynamically on YouTube to retrieve the corresponding audio files."* (p. 4). About 0.5 % may be wrong matches (p. 23).

**Fake songs:** made with Suno and Udio from two inputs, lyrics and song style; *"for each combination of (lyrics, style) input, two songs are generated"* (p. 4).

- **Full Fake:** AI-generated lyrics and style; **Mostly Fake:** AI lyrics based on features of real lyrics, style of a real song; **Half Fake:** real lyrics and the style of a real song (p. 4). Lyrics by GPT-4o, styles of real songs by Gemini 1.5 Flash (pp. 4–5).
- *"Udio models cannot generate songs for lyrics from real songs; thus, HF songs contain only Suno models."* (p. 5).
- Generators and their maximum length (p. 4): Suno v2 80 s, Suno v3 120 s, Suno v3.5 240 s, Udio 32 and Udio 130 s.
- Songs without vocals were removed with PyAnnote (p. 4).

**Names in the files:** the column `algorithm` uses `chirp-v2-xxl-alpha`, `chirp-v3`, `chirp-v3.5`, `udio-30s`, `udio-120s` for Suno v2, v3, v3.5, Udio 32, Udio 130 (the counts match Table 3).

**Numbers – paper vs files:**

| | Paper | Metadata files |
|---|---|---|
| Songs | 97,164 (p. 2) | 97,164 |
| Fake / real songs | 49,074 / 48,090 | 49,074 / 48,090 |
| Fake / real hours | 1,971 / 2,780 (p. 2) | 1,970.6 / 2,780.5 (column `duration`) |
| Average length | 176.03 s (Table 1, p. 2) | 176.0 s |
| Artists of real songs | 9,096 (p. 4) | 9,096 |
| Audio on disk | – | 49,074 fake `.mp3`, **no real songs** |

**Splits** (Table 3, p. 4 – the files give the same numbers):

| Split | Fake | Real | Total | Generators of the fake songs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| train | 34,023 | 32,686 | 66,709 | Suno v3.5, Udio 130 |
| valid | 2,273 | 2,167 | 4,440 | all five |
| test | 12,778 | 13,237 | 26,015 | all five |

- *"the valid and test sets include cases with unseen algorithms (e.g., Suno v2, Suno v3, Udio 32) and unseen singers"*; songs from the same (lyrics, style) input are never in train and in valid / test at the same time (p. 7; checked in the files).
- Class balance: 50.5 % fake in total (51.0 % train, 51.2 % valid, 49.1 % test).

## 9. Protocol (label) files

The labels are in `fake_songs.csv` and `real_songs.csv`, and for each split in `train.csv`, `valid.csv` and `test.csv`. The files contain the lyrics with line breaks inside quoted cells, so one song takes several text lines. The first lines of `fake_songs.csv` and `real_songs.csv`:

```
id,lyrics,algorithm,style,filename,bit_rate,duration,source,lyrics_feature,topic,genre,mood,label,target,skip_time,no_vocal,split
54113,"[Intro]
```

```
filename,title,artist,year,lyrics,duration,youtube_id,label,artist_overlap,target,skip_time,no_vocal,split
real_00000,Rap God,Eminem,2013,"[Intro]
```

| Column | File | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `filename` | both | name of the audio file without `.mp3` | `fake_54113_suno_0`, `real_00000` |
| `label` | both | `full fake`, `half fake`, `mostly fake` or `real` | `full fake` |
| `target` | both | `1` = fake, `0` = real | `1` |
| `split` | both | `train`, `valid` or `test` | `train` |
| `duration` | both | length in seconds | `239.076` |
| `skip_time` | both | instrumental part before the vocals (seconds) | `5.684093750000001` |
| `algorithm` | fake | generator (section 7) | `chirp-v3.5` |
| `source` | fake | `suno` or `udio` | `suno` |
| `style`, `lyrics` | fake | song style and lyrics used to generate the song | `male vocals, lounge, piano, …` |
| `artist`, `title`, `year` | real | artist, title, release year | `Eminem`, `Rap God`, `2013` |
| `youtube_id` | real | YouTube ID of the real song | `EZ-yNVc9Htw` |

- In `train.csv`, `valid.csv`, `test.csv` the column `filepath` gives the path of the audio file (`fake_songs/<filename>.mp3` or `real_songs/<filename>.mp3`).
- The column tables in `README.md` differ from the files: `fake_songs.csv` has no `title`, and `lyrics_features` is called `lyrics_feature`; `real_songs.csv` has no `id`.
